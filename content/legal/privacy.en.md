# Privacy Policy — ISTech

**Effective from:** 2026-10-07


## 1. Who we are

**IGNAULIN SOLUCOES TECNOLOGICAS LTDA** (brand: ISTech), a Brazilian company registered under
CNPJ **49.181.911/0001-51**, is the controller of the personal
data described here, under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018).

## 2. What we collect

This site (https://istechdata.com) does not use cookies or advertising trackers. It uses
Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and reports only aggregated visit statistics
(pages viewed, referrer, country, browser); it does not identify individual visitors. Beyond
that, we only receive the personal data you choose to send us — your **name, email address and
message** — when you use the contact form or write to us by email.

## 3. Why we use it

Only to reply to you and to discuss a possible engagement. Legal basis: steps taken at your
request before a contract (LGPD art. 7, V) and our legitimate interest in answering business
enquiries (LGPD art. 7, IX). We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## 4. Who processes it for us

- Vercel Inc. (USA) — hosts this site.
- Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) — DNS, email routing and Web Analytics (aggregated, cookieless).
- Google LLC (USA) — email.
- Resend, Inc. (USA) — delivers contact-form messages to our inbox.

These providers are in the United States, so your data is transferred internationally,
under LGPD art. 33. Where the transfer relies on standard contractual clauses, we use the
clauses approved by Brazil's ANPD (Resolução CD/ANPD nº 19/2024) or
another basis listed in art. 33; a provider's own terms are not a substitute for them.

### Business contacts and outreach

When we write to a company about our services, we may use a business contact's name, work
email address and role. We take them from the company's public inbox or website (for example
a contact or sales address). Purpose: a short business introduction, nothing else. Legal
basis: legitimate interest (LGPD art. 7, IX). Every message can be answered with "stop" or
"unsubscribe", and we then add the address to a suppression list and do not write again.

## 5. How long we keep it

Messages are kept while the conversation is active and for up to 2 years after the last
contact, unless a contract requires longer (for example, for tax records).

## 6. Your rights

Under LGPD art. 18 you can ask us to confirm whether we hold your data, access it, correct it,
delete it, or learn who we shared it with. Write to **privacy@istechdata.com**. You can
also complain to Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD, www.gov.br/anpd).

## 7. Changes

We will update this page and its effective date whenever this policy changes.
