# Refund Policy — ISTech

**Effective from:** 2026-09-28


## 1. Services under a signed contract

Consulting and engineering services are delivered under a written contract. Cancellation,
refunds and credits for those services follow the terms of that contract.

## 2. Online purchases by consumers (7-day right of withdrawal)

If you are a consumer and bought a product or service from us online, you may withdraw
within **7 (seven) days** of the purchase, without giving a reason, and receive a full refund
of the amount paid, adjusted for inflation (Brazilian Consumer Defense Code, art. 49).

## 3. Undue charges

If you are a consumer and were charged unduly, we refund twice the amount paid in excess,
adjusted for inflation, unless the error is justifiable (Brazilian Consumer Defense Code,
art. 42, sole paragraph).

## 4. How the money is returned

Card payments are refunded to the same card through our payment processor (Stripe). The
time for the credit to appear on your statement depends on your card issuer.

## 5. How to request

Email contact@ignaulin.com with the email address used for the purchase and the date
of the charge.
