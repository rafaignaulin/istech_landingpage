## How contracting works

You contract a company, not an individual. It is the same process as onboarding any international vendor or SaaS supplier, and it goes through procurement, not HR.

1. Agreement. Your standard contractor or vendor agreement with ISTech, or our short MSA and SOW if you prefer. Your governing law is fine.
2. Vendor onboarding. We send company registration details, address, the tax form for your country and bank details for USD, AUD or EUR.
3. Delivery. Remote, on your tooling, under your security and access policies, with an NDA and IP assignment in the agreement.
4. Billing. One invoice per month, paid by international transfer, net 30. No payroll, no benefits, no visa, no employment relationship.

Nothing new is needed on our side. This is how our current clients pay us today.

## Tax and compliance, by country

- United States. We provide a W-8BEN-E. Services are performed outside the US, so there is no withholding and no 1099.
- Australia. No ABN and no GST apply, because the services are delivered from outside Australia. We provide the ATO "Statement by a supplier" form if your finance team asks for it. No withholding applies.
- United Kingdom and European Union. No VAT is charged on the invoice; it falls under the reverse charge rules for services imported from outside the country. No withholding applies.
- Brazil. Invoices are issued as an export of services by ISTech. All local tax obligations are ours.

If your finance team has a specific requirement, send it over. We have not yet found one that could not be met.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do we need a legal entity in your country?** No. You pay an international vendor invoice.

**Can we hire Rafael directly as an employee?** Our engagements run through ISTech. That is what makes the international setup simple for you.

**Who actually does the work?** Rafael, as the named lead on the agreement. We do not swap people in.

**Timezone?** We align to your hours. We have delivered from 15 or more countries across 6 or more timezones for US and Brazilian clients at the same time, without missing delivery.

**Data access and security?** Client data stays on client systems. We work through your VPN, your laptop policy and your access controls, whatever they are. If your client requires access only from a specific country, tell us early and we will confirm whether the engagement fits.

**How fast can we start?** Vendor onboarding usually takes a week on the client side. We can start the day it is done.
